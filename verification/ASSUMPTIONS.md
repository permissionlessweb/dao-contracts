# Assumptions

Pinned while these artifacts were written:

- dao-contracts `5b77af9e91c2c7ec4cb6dfe844e8369d8f10da03` (`feat/calander`)
- cosmwasm `c156aef60266be0cd568a815199984ad283ed2c8`
- Lean `leanprover/lean4:v4.30.0` (`verification/lean/lean-toolchain`)
- Quint 0.32.0, Apalache 0.56.1 as invoked by `quint verify`
- Trailmark 0.2.2

Trusted:

1. The Lean 4 kernel.
2. The hand model in `verification/lean/VotingPow.lean` matches `Uint256::checked_add`, `checked_mul`, `checked_sub`, `saturating_sub`, `full_mul`, and the panicking `Add` and `Sub` at the cosmwasm revision above. The Rust oracle calls `compare_vote_count`, `does_vote_count_pass`, `does_vote_count_fail`, and `Votes::{add_vote,remove_vote,total}` on the closed terms those theorems use, including the 60% complement and a removal that passes the limb. A removal past the limb is `checked_sub` returning `Err`, and `remove_vote` panics on that input.
3. `Decimal` and `Decimal256` from this crate use 18 decimal places. `Threshold::validate` and `assert_valid_percentage_threshold` reject a zero or above-one passing threshold. Quint and Lean still state the overlap witness inside `(0, 1]`, because validation allows every such fraction, not only `Decimal::percent`.
4. Storage, the querier, entrypoints, and reply events. A voting module's power query returns the integer it was given. cw20-stake, cw4, and the bank module are not proved.
5. Trailmark's caller list in `verification/SCOPE.md`. A sink with no caller inside a scanned root is omitted. A missing directory or a preanalysis failure is recorded as `assumed`.
6. Z3, through Apalache, for `tallyIsSum` on traces of length 4 from `init`. Apalache 0.56.1 rejects the same predicate as an inductive invariant because it constrains `yes`, `no`, and `abstain` by inequalities rather than assignments (`yes is used before it is assigned`). The sum for every in-range power is `add_limb_preserves_sum` in Lean. Quint's Rust evaluator rejects integer literals outside i64, so `vote_tally.qnt` runs the same floor formula at precision `100` and denominator `10000` with bound `1000000`. It does not re-check the `10^9` / `10^18` constants; `VotingPow.lean` does.

Specified, and not made total:

- `Votes::add_vote`, `remove_vote`, and `total` use panicking `+` and `-`. `remove_limb_preserves_sum` is the in-range removal. `remove_limb_none` is the panic when the removal exceeds the limb. `votes_total_some` and `votes_total_none` are the panic boundary of the three-limb sum at `2^256`. `MultipleChoiceVotes` uses `checked_add` and `checked_sub` and returns `Err`.
- The delegation-loss query is `effective.saturating_sub(total.saturating_sub(delegated).min(effective))`. With `effective ≤ total`, that loss is the whole delegated amount when total is at most the cap, the partial gap `effective - (total - delegated)` when the removal crosses the cap, and zero when the delegated amount sits entirely above the cap (`delegation_loss_uncapped`, `delegation_loss_partial`, `delegation_loss_zero`). A stale delegated amount above total yields `effective` (`delegation_loss_stale`). `delegation_loss_le_delegated` says this loss never exceeds the delegated amount passed in. `query_loss_keeps_recorded_individual` says a ballot that recorded that same delegated amount keeps its individual power under the `min`. `delegated_reduction_includes_individual` remains the separate arithmetic of `min(7+3, 11)`, which this query cannot return for a delegated amount of 3. `calculate_delegated_vp` is `floor(vp * atomics / 10^18)`, and `delegated_mul_floor_fits` says that floor fits in `Uint256` when `vp` does and the percent atomics are at most `10^18`. `get_voting_power_with_delegation` uses `checked_add` and does not wrap.
- `singlePassed?` and `singleRejected?` follow `SingleChoiceProposal::is_passed` and `is_rejected`: re-voting and the minimum voting period, individual tally before expiry and full tally after, the quorum denominator split, absolute count, and `none` when `total_power - abstain` is out of range. `mcPassed` and `mcRejected` follow the multiple-choice branches, including that an expired standard winner does not consult the unbeatable test. `currentStatus` tests pass before expiry and before rejection.
- The percentage branch of `query_is_active` in cw20-staked, token-staked, and cw721-staked is a checked active-threshold query: `checked_mul` by `10^9`, then `checked_multiply_ratio` (floor division by `10^18`), then `checked_add` of `10^9`, `checked_sub` of one, and `checked_div` by `10^9`. At supply `(2^256 - 1) / 10^9` and atomics `10^18`, that checked add is `none` (`active_checked_round_overflows`). When the checked round returns a value and atomics are at most `10^18`, the value is at most the supply (`active_checked_round_le_supply`). cw721 widens a `Uint128` token count with `full_mul` before the same checked ratio and add. The comments that bound cw20 and bank supply by `2^128` are not premises.
- Condorcet voting power enters the tally only when it is `< 2^128`. `uint256_to_uint128` returns `PowerExceedsTallyRange` otherwise (`condorcet_power_fits_u128`). `add_vote` returns the same error when power exceeds `power_outstanding`. If outstanding power is `< 2^128` and the vote is at most outstanding, the checked subtraction stays in that range (`condorcet_sub_fits`).
- `does_vote_count_pass` compares yes-votes with the passing fraction. `does_vote_count_fail` compares no-votes with `Decimal::one() - percent`. `rejectByThreshold` applies that complement. Five no-votes out of 10 reject a 60% threshold and do not meet 60% itself (`rejection_uses_complement`). Both predicates are true for options `3`, yes `1`, no `2`, and atomics `(10^18 + 2) / 3` on the complement fraction. `Proposal::current_status` matches `is_passed` before `is_rejected`, including when the proposal is also expired, so that state is reported passed. Majority stays disjoint when `yes + no ≤ options`.
